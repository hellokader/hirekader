# Sanity Content Model

## `siteSettings`

Global site identity and contact settings.

Fields: site name, logo, email, WhatsApp, phone, timezone, LinkedIn, default SEO, footer copyright.

## `navigation`

Editable navigation labels and URLs. Presentation and responsive drawer behavior stay in Next.js.

## `footer`

Footer blurb and grouped link columns.

## `service`

Canonical service records such as Google Ads management, Local Services Ads, call and form tracking, and landing page fixes.

Fields: title, slug, summary, SEO.

## `faqGroup`

Grouped FAQ content used on the homepage.

Fields: group title and question/answer rows.

## `blogCategory`

Reusable categories such as Tracking, Google Ads, Local SEO, and Landing pages.

## `blogPost`

Editable blog article content.

Fields: title, slug, excerpt, category, published date, updated date, read time, body, SEO.

## `seo`

Reusable SEO object.

Fields: meta title, meta description, canonical URL, OG image, noindex, nofollow.

## Editor Workflow

Editors should update copy, links, images, FAQs, service summaries, contact details and blog posts in Sanity. Layout, spacing, visual behavior and responsive rules remain in code.
